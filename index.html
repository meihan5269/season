Four Seasons of My Childhood

I grew up in Beijing, China. In this page, I want to talk about the four seasons I remember from my childhood, mostly when I was around 8 to 12 years old. Beijing has four very different seasons, so the weather changed a lot during the year. Each season also gave me different memories from school, family, and spending time with my friends.

Spring

Spring in Beijing was usually cool and pretty dry when I was a child. At the beginning of spring, mornings could still be cold, so I normally wore a jacket when I went to school. Later in the season, the temperature became much warmer. There was not as much rain as in summer, but there were some rainy days. Wind was also very common in Beijing during spring, and sometimes the wind could be really strong.

One thing I remember about spring was spending more time outside after a long winter. When the weather got warmer, I liked going to parks and playing outside with my friends. Beijing sometimes had dusty or sandy days during spring, so the sky did not always look clear. On those days, my family usually told me to close the windows and stay inside more. Even with the wind and dust, I still liked spring because it meant the cold winter was finally ending.

Summer

Summer in Beijing was very hot, especially in July and August. Many days were above 30 degrees Celsius, and some days felt even hotter. Summer also felt more humid than spring. Most of the rain I remember happened during this season. Sometimes there were thunderstorms with heavy rain, lightning, and strong wind. A normal sunny day could suddenly become dark before a storm started.

Summer was probably the most fun season for me because we had summer vacation from school. I spent a lot of time playing games, meeting my friends, and sometimes playing sports outside. When it was too hot, I preferred staying inside with air conditioning. I also remember eating cold drinks and ice cream a lot. Heavy summer rain could make roads have a lot of water, and sometimes my family would wait for the rain to get lighter before going outside.

Fall

Fall was one of my favorite seasons in Beijing because the weather was comfortable. The very hot summer weather started to disappear, and the air became cooler and drier. During the daytime it could still be warm, but mornings and nights were cooler. There was usually less rain than in summer. The sky also seemed clearer on many days, and the leaves slowly changed from green to yellow.

Fall also reminds me of going back to school after summer vacation. At first I could wear a T-shirt during the day, but later I needed a sweater or light jacket. I liked walking outside during this season because it was not too hot or too cold. Sometimes Beijing had strong wind when the weather was changing. Fall always felt kind of short to me because the temperature could drop quickly and winter seemed to arrive very soon.

Winter

Winter in Beijing was cold and very dry. Temperatures could go below 0 degrees Celsius, especially at night. I remember wearing a thick coat, gloves, and sometimes a hat when I went outside. Beijing did get snow, although it did not snow all the time. When snow came, it could cover streets, cars, and trees. Cold wind was also common and made the temperature feel even lower.

As a child, I was always excited when there was enough snow to play outside. My friends and I could make snowballs and play in the snow, even though our hands got cold very fast. I also remember spending more time at home during very cold days. Winter was connected with Chinese New Year too, so I have many family memories from this season. Even though the weather was cold, winter still felt special because of snow, winter break, food, and spending time with my family.
